Perched above a private crescent of shoreline, Aurelia Grand Hotel & Resort has welcomed discerning travelers for nearly a century — with suites dressed in ivory and gold, and service that remembers your name.

A Legacy of Grace

Nearly a century of unhurried hospitality

Since 1928, Aurelia has stood where the limestone cliffs soften into the sea. Our halls have hosted poets, diplomats, and travelers in search of something quieter than the world — a place where mornings begin with espresso on a sunlit terrace and evenings end beneath lantern-lit palms.

Every suite is appointed by hand, every dish composed in season, and every guest received as though returning home. That is not a slogan here; it is the only way we know how to operate.

The Estate

Facilities & Experiences

Eleven acres of terraced gardens, a private crescent of shoreline, and facilities arranged with one
intention — that your hours here feel generously unhurried.

The Infinity Pool

Heated to a perfect 28°C year-round, our edge-less pool appears to pour into the sea below. Sunbeds with ivory parasols line the stone terrace, and the pool bar serves cold-pressed juices, spritzes, and gelato from dawn until the stars arrive.

Heated edge-less design, 25 meters
Private cabanas with service bell
Pool bar & gelato counter
Open daily, 7:00 — 20:00

Spa & Wellness

Carved into the sea caves beneath the estate, the Aurelia Spa is a sanctuary of warm stone, botanical oils, and the distant rhythm of tides. Rituals are designed around your journey — deep-tissue restoration after a long flight, couples' ceremonies of stone and steam, or a single quiet hour of silence.

Six treatment suites & a thermal cave
Signature 90-minute Golden Ritual
Hammam, sauna & vitality pool
Open daily, 9:00 — 21:00

Fitness Atelier

More gym than gymnasium — an atelier of movement with floor-to-ceiling sea views, brass-and-black equipment, and private trainers who design programs around your stay. Morning yoga on the terrace and guided coastal runs are offered daily.

Private trainers & wellness programs
Terrace yoga & coastal guided runs
Recovery lounge & juice bar
Open daily, 6:30 — 21:00

Fine Dining

At Il Giardino, Chef Matteo Rossi translates the coast into ten unhurried courses. A cellar of 1,200 labels, a candlelit main room, and a garden loggia for lunch — with breakfast served each morning on the golden terrace overlooking the sea

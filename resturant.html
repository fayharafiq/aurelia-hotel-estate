The House Table

Cooking the coastline, one course at a time

Il Giardino began in 1976 as the family's private kitchen. Today, under Chef Matteo Rossi — whose career spans Michelin kitchens in Lyon and Milan — it has become a destination in its own right, celebrated for translating the Amalfi coast into ten unhurried courses

Lobster arrives from morning nets. Saffron is sourced from a single hillside farm. The figs come from our own terrace garden. Nothing on the plate travels further than the story it tells.Beneath the restaurant, our sommelier tends a cellar of more than 1,200 labels — a library of vintages assembled over two decades of travel, paired to each course with quiet precision.

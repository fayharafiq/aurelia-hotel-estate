Our Story

A house built on grace

Since 1928

From a family villa to a coastal landmark

Aurelia began as a summer villa belonging to the Rossi-Marchetti family, who opened its doors to weary travelers after the Great War. What began as four guest rooms with linen curtains and lemon trees on the terrace has grown — carefully, deliberately — into one of the coastline's most respected addresses.

Through peace and turbulence, fashion and forgetfulness, the house has kept one promise: that every guest would be received as family, served with discretion, and sent away already planning the return. Today the estate spans eleven acres of terraced gardens, fifty-four suites, and a staff whose average tenure exceeds fourteen years — a rarity we consider our truest luxury.

Our philosophy is simple: time, well spent

We believe hospitality is the art of giving people back their hours — the breakfast that lingers, the walk that slows,
the evening that refuses to end. Everything at Aurelia exists to make time feel generous.

Why Aurelia

What sets this house apart

Family Stewardship

Four generations of the same family have guided this house. Decisions are still made for the next fifty years, not the next quarter.

A Private Stretch of Coast

Guests enjoy a reserved crescent of shoreline with sun loungers, shaded pergolas, and a beachside service bell that always answers.

Craft in Every Detail

Linens pressed by hand, menus written in season, rooms appointed room-by-room. Nothing here is off-the-shelf, and guests feel it.

Service That Remembers

Returning guests are greeted by name, their preferences logged and honored — from pillow firmness to the temperature of their espresso.

Genuine Stillness

No queues, no crowds, no noise beyond the sea. The estate is deliberately sized so that solitude is always within reach.

Discreet Security

A private entrance, unmarked driveways, and staff trained in discretion — the estate has long been trusted by those who value privacy.
